Risk Management Audit Simulator — Daily Loss Limit (No Account Impact)

Independent hypothetical test. Enter realized daily P&L for up to three closed trades. The simulator checks whether a new entry is blocked once the configured daily loss threshold is reached. It does not change the Demo Account.

Default test: -$80, -$70, -$50 = -$200. A new entry should be blocked at the 2% limit.

Risk Management Audit Simulator — Maximum Drawdown (No Account Impact)

Independent hypothetical equity-curve test. After each closed trade, the simulator updates equity and its highest peak, then checks peak-to-trough drawdown. It does not change the Demo Account.

Default test: equity falls from $10,000 to $9,000 (10% drawdown). A new entry should be blocked at the limit.

Position Sizing & Risk-per-Trade Audit — No Account Impact

Independent hypothetical calculator. Quantity is based on cash risk, stop distance and estimated round-trip costs, with a conservative 1x notional cap. Generic units only; it does not place orders or change the Demo Account.

Default example: $10,000 balance × 0.5% risk = $50 risk budget. Entry $2,000, SL $1,990.

SL / TP Testing Simulator — No Account Impact

Hypothetical OHLC test only. It does not open or close demo positions or change balance/history.
